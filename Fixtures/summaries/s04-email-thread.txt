Load test results from last night, as promised.

We simulated 300 scanners hitting the API at once, which is what the Columbus warehouse sees at its busiest hour in December. Response times were fine up to about 180 scanners, then climbed to over 4 seconds. At 250 scanners, about 6% of requests failed outright.

The cause is the inventory lookup, which runs a full table scan for every scan. Adding an index should fix most of it, but I want to rerun the test after the fix, and I do not think we can do that safely and still launch on December 1.

My recommendation is to delay. I would rather launch in January with a system we trust.

Devon

Quick update from the app side.

The good news: the new barcode reader handles damaged labels much better. In the Dayton pilot, failed scans dropped from 7% to under 2%.

The bad news: we found a bug where scans made while a device is offline are sometimes saved twice when it reconnects. It has happened 23 times in the pilot so far. Nobody has been double-charged, because the billing system catches duplicates, but the stock counts are wrong until someone corrects them by hand.

We think we know the cause, but the fix touches the sync code, which is the riskiest part of the app. I'd estimate two to three weeks including testing.

Alex
