Last month I flew from Boston to Lisbon and back for $212, including a carry-on bag. Here is exactly how I did it.

First, I set a fare alert on two different flight search sites in June for any date in October. Prices on this route usually sit around $650. In early August an alert came in at $198 one way on a new budget airline, TransAtlantica, and a $14 return leg appeared the same day as part of a launch sale.

Second, I was flexible. The cheapest dates were a Tuesday out and a Wednesday back, nine days apart. I moved two days of leave to make it work.

Third, I read the baggage rules carefully. The fare included one carry-on of up to 10 kg. A checked bag would have cost $70 each way, which would have wiped out most of the saving, so I packed light.

The catch: the flights left at 6 a.m. and 11 p.m., there were no free drinks, and seats did not recline. For a seven-hour flight that was fine for me but would not be for everyone.

My advice: set alerts early, be flexible with dates, and check the bag fees before you get excited about a cheap fare.
