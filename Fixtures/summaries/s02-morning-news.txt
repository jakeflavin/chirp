Good morning. The kettle is on and the markets are calm. Here is what you need to know before your first meeting.

MARKETS
Stocks closed higher on Monday after a quiet session. The S&P 500 rose 0.4% to 6,812, and the Nasdaq added 0.7%, led by chip makers. Oil fell 2% to $68 a barrel after OPEC said it would raise output in January. The 10-year Treasury yield slipped to 3.9%.

HOUSING
Rents finally cool

The national median rent fell for the fifth month in a row, to $1,690, according to a report from Rentwise released Monday. That is 3.2% lower than a year ago. The biggest drops were in Austin, Phoenix and Nashville, where a wave of new apartment buildings opened over the past two years. Rents in New York and Boston kept rising, though more slowly.

Economists say the trend should help bring overall inflation down, because housing makes up about a third of the consumer price index. But first-time buyers are still stuck: the average 30-year mortgage rate is 6.1%, and home prices are up 4% from last year.

TECH
The smart glasses race heats up

Three companies showed new smart glasses this week. Nordlight's pair costs $399 and can translate a conversation in real time. Verra's glasses have a small display in one lens and last 11 hours on a charge. The third, from Pell, is aimed at cyclists and shows speed and directions. Analysts expect about 9 million smart glasses to sell next year, up from 3 million this year.

The big question is privacy. Each pair has a camera, and lawmakers in the EU have asked for a clear light that shows when it is recording. Nordlight says it will add one in a software update.

WORK
The four-day week trial ends

The UK's largest four-day week trial, with 140 companies, ended last month. A final report says 85% of the companies will keep the shorter week. Staff sick days fell by 40%, and revenue stayed about the same. The companies that dropped the four-day week were mostly in retail and manufacturing, where shifts are harder to cover.

SPORTS
Riverside FC sacks its manager

Riverside FC fired manager Tomas Brandt on Monday after a run of seven games without a win. The club is 17th in the league, one place above the relegation zone. Assistant coach Lena Okafor will take charge for Saturday's game against Harbor City.

AND FINALLY
A library book returned 84 years late

A 1942 edition of a sailing manual was returned to the public library in Dunmore, Pennsylvania, by the grandson of the man who borrowed it. The library waived the fine, which would have been about $3,000.
