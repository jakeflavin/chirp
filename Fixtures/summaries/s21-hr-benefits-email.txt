Hello everyone,

Open enrollment for 2027 benefits is now open and will close on Monday, November 30, at 11:59 PM Eastern. This is the only time of year you can change your health, dental and vision plans unless you have a major life event, such as a new baby or a marriage.

What is changing for 2027

Medical plans: We are keeping the same two medical plans, Core and Plus. The company will continue to pay 90% of the premium for employees and 75% for family members. Because overall health costs rose, your share of the premium for the Plus plan will go up by about $14 a month for employee-only coverage. The Core plan cost stays the same.

New: The Health Savings Account (HSA) company contribution goes up from $500 to $750 a year for employee-only coverage, and from $1,000 to $1,500 for family coverage. You can only use an HSA if you are on the Core plan.

Dental: No changes.

Vision: The frame allowance increases from $150 to $200 every two years.

Mental health: All employees and family members can now have 12 free therapy sessions a year through Calmpath, up from 8. You do not need to enroll to use this; it is available automatically from January 1.

What you need to do

If you want to keep the same plans, you do not need to do anything, except for the HSA and Flexible Spending Account (FSA). These do not roll over: you must re-enroll in them every year, or your contributions will stop on January 1.

To make changes, log in to the benefits portal from the company intranet and choose "2027 Open Enrollment". It takes about 15 minutes. Have the dates of birth and Social Security numbers of any family members you want to add.

Questions?

Join one of our live Q&A sessions on November 16 at 12 PM or November 19 at 4 PM Eastern. Links are on the intranet. You can also email benefits@example-company.com.
