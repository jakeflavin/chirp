Maple Grove Weekly
News for families · November 13, 2026

A note from Principal Davies
Dear families, thank you to everyone who came to parent-teacher conferences last week. We had 412 conferences, the highest number we have ever had. If you missed yours, please email your child's teacher to set up a phone call before Thanksgiving.

Picture retake day is Thursday, November 19
If your child was absent on picture day or you would like a retake, send them to school on November 19 with the original photo packet. Children who are new to school since October can also have their photo taken that day.

Thanksgiving break
There is no school from Wednesday, November 25, through Friday, November 27. The after-school program is also closed on those days. School starts again on Monday, November 30.

Winter coat drive
The PTA is collecting new and gently used coats, hats and gloves for children's sizes 4 to 14. Please leave donations in the box by the front office before December 4. Last year we gave away 230 coats to families in our community.

Change to car line
Because of construction on Elm Street, the car line will enter from Pine Avenue starting Monday, November 16. Please do not stop on Elm Street to drop children off. Crossing guards will be at the corner of Pine and 3rd from 7:45 a.m.

Fourth grade field trip
Fourth graders will visit the Science Discovery Center on Wednesday, December 9. Permission slips and the $12 fee are due by December 1. Families who need help with the fee can contact the front office confidentially. We need six parent volunteers; sign up with Ms. Ortega.

Lunch menu
Next week's lunch menu is attached. Pizza Friday is back!
